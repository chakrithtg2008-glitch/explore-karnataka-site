Introduction to Karnataka

Karnataka is a beautiful state in southern India, known for its rich cultural heritage, historical monuments, natural beauty and modern cities. Bengaluru is the capital city of Karnataka.

The state is famous for its ancient temples, royal palaces, green forests, scenic hill stations, waterfalls and traditional Kannada culture. Karnataka offers memorable experiences for every traveller.

Popular Tourist Places

🏙️

Bengaluru

Explore the Garden City, Lalbagh Botanical Garden, Cubbon Park and Bengaluru Palace.

🏰

Mysuru

Visit the magnificent Mysuru Palace, Chamundi Hills and the famous Brindavan Gardens.

🏖️

Gokarna

Enjoy beautiful beaches, coastal scenery and peaceful surroundings along the Arabian Sea.

🛕

Hampi

Discover impressive ruins, ancient temples and remarkable stone architecture from the Vijayanagara era.

Historical Places

🏛️

Mysuru Palace

A grand royal palace famous for its architecture, decorations and royal heritage.

🗿

Hampi Monuments

Explore the historic temples and monuments of the former Vijayanagara Empire.

🛕

Belur Temple

Admire the intricate stone carvings and beautiful Hoysala architecture at Chennakeshava Temple.

🪨

Badami Caves

Visit ancient rock-cut cave temples known for their carvings and historic significance.

Waterfalls & Hill Stations

🌊

Jog Falls

One of India's famous waterfalls, located in the lush Western Ghats of Karnataka.

⛰️

Coorg

Enjoy misty hills, coffee plantations, forests and beautiful natural scenery.

🌿

Chikkamagaluru

Famous for coffee estates, mountain views and trekking destinations such as Mullayanagiri.

💦

Abbey Falls

A scenic waterfall near Madikeri, surrounded by greenery and coffee plantations.

Famous Karnataka Foods

🥞

Mysore Masala Dosa

A popular crispy dosa with a spicy filling, usually served with chutney and sambar.

🍚

Bisi Bele Bath

A traditional Karnataka dish prepared with rice, lentils, vegetables and aromatic spices.

🍮

Mysore Pak

A famous sweet made mainly from gram flour, ghee and sugar.

🥣

Ragi Mudde

A traditional ragi-based dish commonly enjoyed with sambar or other curries.

Travel Information

✈️ By Air: Bengaluru and Mangaluru are among the airports serving travellers to Karnataka.

🚆 By Train: Major cities such as Bengaluru, Mysuru and Hubballi are connected by railway services.

🚌 By Bus: State and private bus services connect many cities and tourist destinations.

📅 Best Time to Visit: October to March is generally pleasant for many destinations. Weather varies by region and season.

🎒 Travel Tip: Check local weather, transport schedules, entry fees and attraction timings before travelling.
